## Catalog

### oBike — A perfect app can still be useless
https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e0b205df-1211-449d-880d-7eba85171074

> A bike-sharing app can work perfectly and still be useless. Open the map. No bike nearby. That is not an app problem. It is a distribution problem. At oBike, we had to make the service real on the ground: where bikes went, who allowed them there, and whether people could find one when they needed it. The lesson for founders? Before buying attention, ask what must already be in place for your product to work. Distribution is part of the product.

### BIGO — You cannot copy local trust
https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/12368a7f-9bf5-42c2-8e47-06435f1461ed

> The app can cross a border in seconds. A local team cannot. At BIGO, I worked on growth across Southeast Asia and the Middle East. In the Middle East, that included government relationships and building a local team. The app was global. The work around it had to be local. That is what glocalisation meant in practice. Before entering another country, ask who needs to believe in the product before users will. Distribution is part of the product.

### Your bio is a label
https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/94010830-1906-42f4-a26f-029add798e71

> Everyone writes "passionate storyteller". Recruiters skim these. Tell one story instead: one moment you changed, in three lines — what broke, what you did, what changed. Pin it above the label. Now the label is earned.

### Endowed progress — Show people what they have already done
https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a4492770-6793-41aa-a7d0-18df8cfd3a02

> Two cards, same reward. One had ten spaces, the other had ten with two stamped free. About 19% finished the empty card; 34% finished the pre-stamped one. The endowed progress effect: show the completed step, say what it unlocked, point to the next step.

### Make the service real on the ground (oBike, field series)
https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d16f0584-16dd-4225-8499-75603370289d

> A perfect app can still be a useless service, and you cannot fix that with a button. Before the ads: bikes in place, permits signed, easy to find one. First, build the conditions.
